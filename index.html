What we're actually fixing

He has a lure, not a recall

He comes to the treat, not to the word. The cue has never predicted anything on its own, so it's noise. That's the whole problem.

The fix is the order of operations

Empty hands. Say the word once. He arrives. Then food appears. Showing food first is what built the lure and it's the habit that has to die.

The air can is retired

It did its job at the door and the phrase holds on its own now. Leave it there — no new behaviors built that way, and nothing like it goes near the recall.

Set up

Chico's week

Timing matters more than volume. He can't learn anything the evening after daycare.

Daycare days
×3
One 10-minute session in the morning before drop-off. Nothing at night — he's cooked. Same facility every week, no rotating.
Home days
Two 10-minute recall sessions. Plus one distance session with other dogs in view.
Weekends
Yard work on the long line once you're past week 4. Sniff walks in quiet places.
Every walk
Five minutes of sit, down and hand targets in the driveway before you move. Take the edge off where it's boring.
