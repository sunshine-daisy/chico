Rules for every exercise

  1. Say it once. Never repeat a cue. If he doesn't respond, the setup was too hard.
  2. Empty hands. Food lives in the pouch. It appears after he does the thing.
  3. Read him first. Not mid-scratch, mid-sniff or while eating. Wait three seconds.
  4. Pay every time. Real food, several small pieces for a recall.
  5. Stop before he's bored. Ten minutes max. End on a win.
